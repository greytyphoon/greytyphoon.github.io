Likes the study of magic, but not the pressure his academic familiy puts unto him. Finds inner peace in practicing fencing, too.

It's somewhat ambiguous if Distracting Spellstrike works with Eye for Number to Feint using Society with both a +1 circ and +2 status bonus. It should, because Distracting Spellstrike only forbids changing the effect of a Feint, but it remains debatable. If it does, grab a Choker of Elocution for the +1 item bonus and become amazing at feinting! It's so good that you'll want to feint against off-guard foes because your status bonus becomes absurd and your chances to crit are great.
