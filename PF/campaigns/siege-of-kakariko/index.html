The Siege of Kakariko

Player Guide

Character creation

  • Start at 6th level, with 0 xp.
  • Free Archetype
    ➥ only for the Lastwall Sentry dedication,
    ➥ or one from Paths of Hyrule: Player Core.
  • Ancestry Paragon.
  • Automatic Bonus Progression.
  • Start with an item of each level:
    ➥ 6th, 5th, 4th, 3rd, 2nd, and 1st.
  • Start with 80gp.
  • Please check with the GM before using elements that aren't [Common].

Setting

  • Hyrule.
  • Urban environment.
  • Low magic.
  • Medieval technology.
  • Primitive firearms.
  • Limited divine involvement.
  • Hylian pantheon.
  • Uncommon ancestries cause villagers to react with surprise but not hostility.
  • Avoid Goblin, Orc, Blin, Lizardfolk, Lizal, and other ancestries associated with the Demon King.

Allowed 3rd-party material

  • Paths of Hyrule: Player Core by Aoife Ester
  • Paths of Hyrule: GM Core by Aoife Ester
  • Reflavoring official Paizo resources is also allowed. Ancestries, backgrounds, deities, etc.

The story so far

Hyrule needs heroes!

The King of the Gerudo has overthrown the Hyrulean Royal Family. Castle Town has recently fallen, and is overrun by his army of evil. The Princess and the Hero of Legends have both disappeared. Still, Kakariko village stands, housing brave hylian survivors. At the horizon, Ganondorf's army awaits any sign of weakness to strike. The people must take their fate in their own hands!

You are brave soldiers working to defend the walls of your last bastion.

What evil plot will you have to face next?
