The Siege of Kakariko

Player Guide

Character creation

  • Start at 6th level, with 0 xp.
  • Free Archetype
    ➥ only for the Lastwall Sentry dedication.
  • Ancestry Paragon.
  • Automatic Bonus Progression.
  • Start with an item of each level:
    ➥ 6th, 5th, 4th, 3rd, 2nd, and 1st.
  • Start with 80gp.
  • Please check with the GM before using elements that aren't [Common].

Setting

  • Hyrule.
  • Urban environment.
  • Low magic.
  • Medieval technology.
  • Primitive firearms.
  • Limited divine involvement.
  • Hylian pantheon.
  • Ancestries other than Hylian, Goron, Zora, or Gerudo are surprising.

Allowed 3rd-party material

  • Paths of Hyrule: Player Core by Aoife Ester

The story so far

Hyrule needs heroes!

The King of the Gerudo has overthrown the Hyrulean Royal Family. Castle Town has recently fallen, and is overrun by his army of evil. The princess and the Hero of legends have disappeared. Kakariko village still stands, housing brave hylian survivors. At the horizon, Ganondorf's army awaits a weakness in your defenses. The people must take their fate in their own hands!

You are brave soldiers working to defend the walls of your last bastion.

What evil plot will you thwart next?
