Player Guide

Character creation

  • Homebrew, rules-lite system, loosely powered by the apocalypse.
  • Don't prepare backstories, inventory, or even names.
  • Prepare your prefered conditions and stats see below.

Setting

  • Our world
  • Urban environment.
  • No magic.
  • Modern technology.
  • Modern firearms.
  • No divine involvement.

Conditions

Choose four. They act as your HP: when you take damage, you will activate one of your conditions, chosen randomly. You can clear them by acting them out during roleplay. One of your teammates must then reach out to you, and you'll clear the condition at the end of the scene. You can only clear one condition per scene: this typically means that a new event or obstacle must be introduced before clearing more.

Afraid
Cleared by running away from a challenge.
Angry
Cleared by lashing out against a teammate.
Betrayed
Cleared by refusing to believe a teammate.
Defeatist
Cleared by wallowing in self-pity and refusing to attempt a challenge.
Envious
Cleared by taking something, ideally physically, from a teammate.
Insecure
Cleared by sharing a secret or being vulnerable with a teammate.
Jealous
Cleared by lying to a teammate.
Proud
Cleared by attempting something risky alone, refusing help.
Sarcastic
Cleared by flippantly blowing off a teammate.
Tired
Cleared by taking an inconvenient break.
Vain
Cleared by fishing for compliments.

Stats

There are three stats. When confronted with a challenge, you will roll 2d6 and add one of these stats. A result of nine is typically a "success at a cost", above is a success, below is a failure. Your stats must add up to seven and must each be between zero and four.

  • Athletics, your physical abilities, strength, finesse, dexterity, reflexes;
  • Logic, your analytic focus, creativity, intelligence, knowledge, smarts;
  • Intuition, your deeper self, charisma, passion, wisdom, heart, guts.

Extra

Once your have chosen your Conditions and Stats, choose one of the following bonuses.

  • Gain two extra points for your stats, you can divide them or not, and you can go above the usual limit for stats.
  • Gain the Serene condition, which is cleared without any particular effort, as your fifth condition.

Wounds

During the game, you may gain wounds. Each one is placed on top of a condition: as long as you are wounded, that condition is unavailable to you, effectively lowering your max HP. When you gain the wound, you choose which condition it covers. Wounds are much more difficult to clear, can have specific drawbacks (e.g. you limp), and aren't limited to physical ailments: they can be long-lasting mental distress.

Helping, Bonuses, and Maluses

Sometimes, when using an appropriate tool or in an advantageous situation, you may get a +1 or even +2. Similarly, shoddy tools and bad circumstances may give -1 or -2. A teammate who's helping can add half their stat as well, rounded down. Helping implies that they can't attempt the roll themselves, and they should typically use the same stat to help.

A single roll can be affected only once by each type of modifier. For example, if two teammates are helping, add the higher bonus; if a teammate is helping and you have the perfect item for the job, add both bonuses.
