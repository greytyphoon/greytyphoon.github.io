Player Guide

Character creation

  • Homebrew, rules-lite system, loosely powered by the apocalypse.
  • Don't prepare backstories, or even names.
  • Prepare your prefered conditions and stats see below.

Setting

  • Our world
  • Urban environment.
  • No magic.
  • Modern technology.
  • Modern firearms.
  • No divine involvement.

Conditions

Choose 4. They act as your HP: when you take damage, you will become one of your conditions, chosen randomly. You can clear them by acting them out. One of your teammates must then reach out to you, and you'll clear the condition at the end of the scene.

Afraid
Cleared by running away from a challenge.
Angry
Cleared by lashing out against a teammate.
Betrayed
Cleared by refusing to believe a teammate.
Defeatist
Cleared by wallowing in self-pity and refusing to attempt a challenge.
Envious
Cleared by taking something, ideally physically, from a teammate.
Insecure
Cleared by sharing a secret or being vulnerable with a teammate.
Jealous
Cleared by lying to a teammate.
Proud
Cleared by attempting something risky alone, refusing help.
Sarcastic
Cleared by flippantly blowing off a teammate.
Tired
Cleared by taking an inconvenient break.
Vain
Cleared by fishing for compliments.

Stats

There are three stats. When confronted with a challenge, you will roll 2d6 and add one of these stats. A result of 6-7 is a "success at a cost", above is a success, below is a failure. Your stats must add up to one and cannot be higher than two or lower than minus two.

  • Body, your physical abilities, strength, finesse, dexterity;
  • Mind, your analytic focus, creativity, intelligence, knowledge;
  • Heart, your deeper self, charisma, passion, wisdom, guts.

Quirk

Once your have chosen your Stats and Conditions, choose one of the following bonuses.

  • Gain two extra points for your stats, you can divide them or not, and you can go above the limit of two.
  • Gain the Serene condition, which clears out automatically, as your fifth condition.

Wounds

During the game, you may gain wounds. Each one is placed on top of a condition: as long as you are wounded, that condition is unavailable to you, effectively lowering your max HP. When you gain the wound, you choose which condition it covers. Wounds are much more difficult to clear, can have specific drawbacks (e.g. you limp), and aren't limited to physical ailments: they can be long-lasting mental distress.
